FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you build smart contracts from scratch?
Yes. I write, test, and deploy custom Solidity or Rust smart contracts for EVM‑compatible chains. I also perform gas optimizations and provide audit‑ready code.
What back‑end frameworks do you prefer?
I'm equally comfortable with Express, Fastify, and serverless Next.js API routes. I choose based on the project's scale and performance requirements.
Can you integrate Web3 auth (e.g. MetaMask) into existing apps?
Absolutely. I've integrated wallet connections, transaction signing, and on‑chain identity into dozens of projects. It's a standard part of my workflow.
Do you offer post‑launch support for smart contracts?
Yes. Every engagement includes a post‑launch support period. I also offer monitoring and upgrade strategies for immutable contracts.
What are your typical project timelines?
Timelines vary by complexity. A high-conversion landing page typically takes 1-2 weeks, while a complex full-stack dApp or SaaS platform can take 4-8 weeks from discovery to delivery.
How do you handle pricing?
I primarily work on a fixed-project basis to ensure transparency and alignment on deliverables. For ongoing consulting or maintenance, I offer flexible monthly retainers.
Are you currently available for new projects?
I typically book projects 2-4 weeks in advance. However, I always leave room for smaller consultations or high-priority launches. Reach out to check my current window.
Do you work solo or with a team?
I operate as an independent specialist, which means you get direct access to my expertise. For massive-scale projects, I have a trusted network of auditors and designers I can bring in.
Do you offer UI/UX redesigns for existing platforms?
Yes. I specialize in taking legacy or "engineer-styled" platforms and transforming them into modern, high-performance experiences that prioritize conversion and clarity.
